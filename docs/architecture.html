Solution Overview

Happier Robot is an end-to-end autonomous AI sales agent system that handles the entire B2B sales cycle — from first contact to deal closure — without manual intervention. Six production workflows on HappyRobot orchestrate inbound/outbound calls, emails, scheduling, and human-in-the-loop escalation. A self-learning brain powered by Cognee stores and recalls lead context, negotiation patterns, and product knowledge, making the agent smarter with every interaction. Quality is enforced by 50 northstars, 15 adversarial tests, and strict negotiation guardrails.
