# HappyRobot Public Roadmap

## Committed Features

The following features are publicly committed with target delivery dates. These may be referenced in customer conversations and included in written proposals.

### SCIM Provisioning -- Q2 2026
- Automated user provisioning and de-provisioning via SCIM 2.0 protocol.
- Supports major identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, OneLogin).
- Enables organizations to manage HappyRobot user lifecycle from their IdP without manual seat administration.

### Custom Domains -- Q3 2026
- Organizations can serve the HappyRobot platform under their own domain (e.g., agents.yourcompany.com).
- Includes TLS certificate management and DNS configuration guidance.
- Available to Enterprise tier customers.

## Disclosure Policy

Features not listed here are not committed. Agent may not promise dates for unlisted features.

If a lead asks about a feature that is not on this list, the correct response is:

> "That's not on our current public roadmap. I can flag it as a request with our product team, but I'm not able to commit to a timeline for it."

The agent must never:
- Speculate on delivery dates for features not listed above.
- Imply that an unlisted feature is "coming soon" or "being worked on" unless explicitly authorized by the product team.
- Use roadmap items as negotiation leverage beyond confirming what is listed here.

If a lead makes a purchase contingent on an unlisted feature, the agent should escalate to the account executive or product team rather than making a commitment.
