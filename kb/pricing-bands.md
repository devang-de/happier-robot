# HappyRobot Pricing Bands

## Tier Structure

| Tier       | Price          | Seats        | Billing           | Commitment        |
|------------|----------------|--------------|--------------------|-------------------|
| Starter    | $49/seat/mo    | Up to 10     | Annual or monthly  | None required     |
| Growth     | $39/seat/mo    | 11 - 100     | Annual or monthly  | None required     |
| Enterprise | $29/seat/mo    | 100+         | Annual only        | Annual commitment required |

### Tier Details

**Starter ($49/seat/month)**
- Designed for small teams evaluating or running initial deployments.
- Maximum of 10 seats.
- Full platform access: all channels (voice, SMS, email, WhatsApp), workflow builder, knowledge bases, integrations.
- Standard support (email, business hours).

**Growth ($39/seat/month)**
- Volume break for scaling teams with 11 to 100 seats.
- Everything in Starter, plus priority support and dedicated onboarding.
- No annual commitment required, but annual billing is standard.

**Enterprise ($29/seat/month)**
- For organizations with 100 or more seats.
- Annual commitment is required -- monthly billing is not available on Enterprise.
- Everything in Growth, plus SSO/SAML enforcement, audit log exports, dedicated account management, and custom SLA options.

## Discount Authorization Rules

All discounts are measured as a percentage off the list price for the applicable tier.

### GREEN Zone: Up to 10% Discount
- The sales agent **may approve** discounts up to and including 10% without additional authorization.
- No escalation required.
- Common use: volume incentive for near-boundary seat counts, competitive match for known alternatives, early commitment incentive.

### YELLOW Zone: 10% to 25% Discount
- Discounts between 10% and 25% (exclusive of 10%, inclusive of 25%) **require pricing team sign-off** before the agent may commit.
- The agent must not verbally commit to a YELLOW-zone discount on a call. Instead, the agent should acknowledge the ask, state that approval is needed, and commit to a follow-up timeline.
- Common use: large enterprise deals, multi-year commitments, strategic accounts.

### RED Zone: Above 25% Discount
- Discounts above 25% are **never allowed**, regardless of deal size, strategic value, or competitive pressure.
- The agent must decline RED-zone requests cleanly and without ambiguity.
- There is no escalation path for RED-zone discounts -- they are non-negotiable.

## Payment Terms

- **Annual billing** is the standard arrangement across all tiers.
- **Monthly billing** is available on request for Starter and Growth tiers. Monthly billing does not change the per-seat price.
- Enterprise tier requires annual billing; monthly billing is not available.
- Payment is due net-30 from invoice date unless otherwise negotiated in writing.

## Contract Duration

- Standard contracts are 12-month (annual) terms.
- Multi-year terms (24-month, 36-month) are available and may be used as leverage in discount negotiations.
- **No fixed-term pricing above 3 years.** The agent must not offer or agree to contract terms longer than 36 months.

## Important Rules for Agents

1. Always quote list price first before discussing any discount.
2. Discounts apply to the per-seat rate, not to add-on services or overages.
3. Never stack discounts (e.g., volume discount + competitive match + multi-year discount as separate line items).
4. If a lead requests pricing outside of these bands, escalate to the pricing team.
5. All quoted prices are in USD unless explicitly agreed otherwise.
